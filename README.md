## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Structure

> 💡 **Market Size & Structure**: The global Airline Management & Flight Operations Software market is estimated at **$5.2 Billion - $6.8 Billion (2026)**, expanding at a CAGR of ~8.5%. The market structure is **moderately-to-highly concentrated** in the enterprise tier (dominated by legacy incumbents like Amadeus, Sabre, Lufthansa Systems, and Boeing/Jeppesen), while remaining **moderately fragmented** in the charter, business aviation, and flight training segments (served by agile cloud-native providers like Leon Software, FL3XX, and FlightLogger).
