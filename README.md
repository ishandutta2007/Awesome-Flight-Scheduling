# Awesome-Flight-Scheduling

# Top Flight Scheduling & Operations Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Airline & Charter Scheduling, Dispatch, Crew Pairing, Flight Planning & Day-of-Operations Control*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Flight Scheduling and Operations**. These systems cover schedule construction, aircraft and crew assignment, flight planning, dispatch, ops control, and recovery for airlines, charter operators, and aircraft management companies.

**Examples** include Leon Software, FlightLogger, FL3XX, Skybook, AIMS Airline Software, Awery, ForeFlight Dispatch, NAVBLUE N-Flight Planning / N-Ops, Jeppesen OpsControl, FLY Online Tools, Lufthansa Systems NetLine, FLYR, Sabre Schedule Manager, Amadeus Schedule Recovery, IBS iFlight, Merlot Aero, Airpas, and RAIDO (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Certified airline operations and scheduling systems are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options are limited to virtual-airline management, flight-log tools, and experimental ops prototypes. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the large commercial gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Leon Software](https://leonsoftware.com/)**  
  Cloud-based flight operations and scheduling platform popular with charter, business aviation, and aircraft management companies for scheduling, crew, and dispatch workflows.

- **[FlightLogger](https://flightlogger.net/)**  
  Flight training and operations management software used by flight schools and smaller operators for scheduling, logging, and compliance.

- **[FL3XX](https://www.fl3xx.com/)**  
  Aviation management platform covering flight operations, scheduling, crew, maintenance, and charter sales for business aviation.

- **[Skybook / related EFB and ops tools](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Electronic flight bag and operational briefing solutions used in dispatch and crew workflows.

- **[AIMS Airline Software](https://www.aims.aero/)**  
  Integrated airline operations system covering scheduling, crew management, operations control, and related modules for commercial airlines.

- **[Awery Aviation Software](https://awery.aero/)**  
  Aviation ERP and operations suite for airlines and operators covering scheduling, ops, and commercial functions.

- **[ForeFlight Dispatch](https://foreflight.com/)**  
  Dispatch and flight-planning capabilities within the ForeFlight ecosystem for professional aviation operations.

- **[NAVBLUE (N-Flight Planning, N-Ops, Crew)](https://www.navblue.aero/)**  
  Airbus company providing flight planning, operations control, and crew solutions for airlines.

- **[Jeppesen OpsControl and related](https://ww2.jeppesen.com/)**  
  Boeing company solutions for operations control, flight planning, and crew in airline environments.

- **[Lufthansa Systems NetLine, Sabre, Amadeus, IBS iFlight, Merlot, FLYR, Airpas, RAIDO and other enterprise suites](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Major airline IT providers offering schedule management, recovery, crew pairing, and day-of-ops systems for large carriers.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[YAAMS (Yet Another Airline Management System)](https://github.com/YAAMSOrg/yaams-server)**  
  Open-source virtual airline management platform for tracking PIREPs, fleets, and community operations (not for real-world certified ops).

- **[OpenFlights](https://github.com/jpatokal/openflights)**  
  Open project for storing personal flight history, mapping routes, and providing free airport/airline/route datasets.

- **[FlightAdmin and experimental ops prototypes](https://github.com/flightsadmin/flightadmin)**  
  Community flight operations management concepts covering scheduling, load control, and related modules (not production airline certified).

- **[Flight planning open libraries and calculators](https://github.com/)**  
  Open tools for performance, fuel, and route calculations used in research or non-certified contexts.

- **[Crew pairing and rostering open research codes](https://github.com/)**  
  Academic and research implementations of crew optimization algorithms (not turnkey airline systems).

- **[Schedule recovery open optimization prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Experimental solvers for disruption management and aircraft/crew re-assignment.

- **[EFB and briefing open document tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Open templates and generators for operational flight plans and crew packages in non-certified settings.

- **[Airport and airline open data projects](https://github.com/)**  
  Datasets and APIs for airports, runways, and routes useful for planning prototypes.

- **[Simulation and training open flight ops tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Tools aimed at flight schools and virtual operations rather than Part 121/135 production.

- **[Documentation and aviation data open resources](https://github.com/)**  
  Community guides and standards references for understanding commercial flight ops systems.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **YAAMS** or similar for virtual airlines and simulation communities.
- Leveraging **OpenFlights** data and personal log tools for analysis and visualization.
- Accepting that regulatory-compliant scheduling, dispatch release, crew legality, ops control, and recovery for real airlines and charter operators still require commercial platforms (Leon, FL3XX, AIMS, NAVBLUE, Jeppesen, NetLine, Sabre, Amadeus, IBS, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on education, research, and non-certified operations only.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: For real operations, commercial certified systems are the norm. For research or virtual airlines: model schedules and constraints in open optimizers → simulate with open data → log flights in open tools. Suitable only for non-certified or academic use. Production airlines and professional operators rely on commercial flight scheduling and operations platforms.
